Frequently Asked

Quick Answers

What is the MOQ?

Minimum order is 1 container (18 MT) for most products. For private label and custom packaging, the same MOQ applies.

How long is production?

Standard production time is 2–3 weeks after order confirmation and deposit. Peak season may extend to 4 weeks.

What payment terms do you accept?

We accept Telegraphic Transfer (T/T) with 30% deposit and 70% against copy of B/L. LC at sight available for established buyers.

Can I get samples before ordering?

Yes, we provide free samples (up to 2 kg) for serious buyers. Shipping cost is borne by the buyer.
