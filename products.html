Technical Specifications

Standard Quality Parameters

All products are tested in our in-house laboratory before shipment. Third-party inspection available on request.

Moisture Content

< 8%

Maximum moisture to ensure high calorific value and prevent mold during shipping.

Ash Content

< 5%

Low ash means less residue, easier cleanup, and better user experience for end customers.

Fixed Carbon

> 75%

High fixed carbon ensures longer burn time and consistent heat output.

Calorific Value

7,000+ kcal/kg

High heat output suitable for both domestic grilling and industrial applications.

Volatile Matter

< 15%

Controlled volatile matter for safe ignition and minimal flare-up.

Ignition Time

8–12 min

Quick and consistent ignition for Shisha cubes and BBQ briquettes.
