About Java Briquettes

Indonesia's Trusted Charcoal Exporter

From a small family operation to an export company serving 30+ countries — built on consistency, transparency, and premium quality.

Our Story

13 years of refining fire.

Java Briquettes Co. started in 2013 as a small charcoal trading business in Surabaya, East Java. Over the years, we built direct relationships with coconut shell processors in Central and East Java, and hardwood charcoal producers across the archipelago.

Today, we operate our own production facility and quality control laboratory, exporting over 120 containers annually to markets in Europe, the Middle East, Asia and the Americas.

Our mission is simple: deliver charcoal that meets your exact specifications, shipment after shipment, with transparent pricing and reliable logistics from Tanjung Perak port.
